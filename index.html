About

I'm a digital journalist and editor with more than 10 years of experience creating editorial and branded content across travel, lifestyle, entertainment, food, culture and breaking news. I've developed original stories, managed editorial teams, written branded articles, edited video and produced content for streaming and social platforms.

At Hearst Television's Very Local, I created branded content supporting partnerships with iHeartMedia, Delish, Woman's Day and local businesses. Earlier, at MyArea Network, I collaborated with the NFL on Pro Bowl branded content and event promotion. I'm most drawn to travel and lifestyle journalism and to creative partnerships that help audiences discover something new.

Specialties Digital journalism · Editorial leadership · Branded content · Travel and lifestyle · Multimedia production · SEO · Audience development · Content strategy
