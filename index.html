About LeeAnn

Experience

Over the past 10+ years, I've developed and produced editorial and branded content across travel, lifestyle, entertainment, food, culture and breaking news: developing original stories, managing editorial teams, writing branded articles, editing video, creating digital graphics and producing content for streaming and social platforms.

At Hearst Television's Very Local, I helped triple organic traffic in the first year, increase organic search performance by 53% and contribute to 42% year-over-year growth in app downloads. I led a network of 10 to 20 freelance contributors and created branded content supporting partnerships with iHeartMedia, Delish, Woman's Day and local businesses. Earlier, at MyArea Network, I collaborated with the NFL on Pro Bowl branded content and event promotion.
