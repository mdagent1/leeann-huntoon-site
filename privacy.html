Privacy

Last updated November 12, 2026

The short version

This site doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and it doesn't collect anything about you unless you choose to send a message.

Contact form

When the contact form is live, the name, email address and message you enter are sent through Web3Forms, a form-delivery service, to LeeAnn's inbox so she can reply. They aren't used for anything else or shared with anyone else. Web3Forms handles that delivery under its own privacy policy.

Fonts and hosting

The site's fonts are stored on this site, so loading a page doesn't contact Google or any other font service. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, processes basic technical information such as your IP address to deliver the pages.

Links to articles

The article cards link to the outlets that published each story. Those sites have their own privacy policies and may use cookies or analytics once you visit them.
